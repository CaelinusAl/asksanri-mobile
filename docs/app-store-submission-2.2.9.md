## 6. Resolution Center Reply (Apple Reviewer'a)

**English (önerilen — Apple reviewer'lar uluslararası):**

```
Hello,

Thank you for the previous review. We have substantially redesigned the app in build 2.2.9 (build number 12) to address the concerns raised:

1) Guideline 4.3(b) — Saturated Category
We have repositioned the app away from numerology / astrology framing entirely. The iOS surface is now a focused mindfulness daily reminder:
- The home screen shows a single reflective sentence per day, chosen from a curated bank of mindfulness prompts. There is no horoscope, no zodiac sign reading, no numerology calculation, and no fortune-telling content visible in the iOS app.
- The category has been changed to Health & Fitness (primary) and Lifestyle (secondary).
- App name, subtitle, description and keywords have all been rewritten around mindfulness, journaling, and reflection.

2) Guideline 5.1.1(v) — Forced Sign-In
The forced redirect to the login screen has been removed. The full core experience now runs anonymously:
- First launch shows an onboarding flow (tone, time, language, optional notifications).
- The Daily screen, the AI reflection chat, the local Echo Archive and the Settings screen all work without any account.
- Sign-in is now offered only inside the optional premium upsell, where it is genuinely required to associate a purchase with an account across devices.

3) Guideline 3.1.1 — In-App Purchase
All paid features remain delivered through Apple In-App Purchase via RevenueCat. The previous hardcoded TRY (₺) currency strings have been replaced with Apple's localized priceString or with neutral "VIP" labels on iOS, so the iOS UI no longer suggests any payment outside of IAP.

We believe the app now fits squarely within Apple's mindfulness category and removes all of the friction the previous review surfaced. We would be grateful for a fresh review.

Thank you for your time.

— The Sanrı team
```

**Türkçe (kendi notların için, Apple'a İngilizcesi gönderilir):**
